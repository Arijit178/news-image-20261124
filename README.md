A front-page poster summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news
in one 1024x1024 image, for the make.fun bounty
"Sum up 24 November's world news in one image"
(news-image-20261124).

`draw.py` draws `entry.png` with Python/Pillow and DejaVu fonts
(no photos, no copied art). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies `entry.png` byte for byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`).

## Run

```sh
python3 draw.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## The eight stories

1. **Strait of Hormuz: Iran says four tankers hit mines, strait closed further.**
   The strait stays closed until Washington meets Tehran's conditions.
   Source: Daily Summary of World, 24 Nov 2026.
2. **The Hague: ICJ finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya in 2017.**
   The court rules 9-6 — the first time the UN's highest court has found
   a state responsible for genocide. Source: Daily Summary / ICJ, 24 Nov 2026.
3. **Gaza: Israel kills Hamas internal security chief Brig. Gen. Naeem Abu Nahel.**
   Six others are killed in the Gaza strike. Source: Reuters / Daily Summary,
   24 Nov 2026.
4. **Space / NATO: NATO chief Mark Rutte vows defence against a space threat.**
   Reports say Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite, dubbed Sputnik-S,
   within 60 days. Source: BBC / German media, 24 Nov 2026.
5. **Zaporizhzhia, Ukraine: overnight Russian attack kills 2, wounds 40.**
   Four children are among the wounded; an apartment building is hit.
   Source: Just Security Early Edition, 24 Nov 2026 / Kyiv Independent.
6. **Villavicencio, Colombia: car bomb outside regional government offices injures 23.**
   The attack comes on the eve of the 10th anniversary of the FARC peace deal.
   Source: Daily Summary, 24 Nov 2026.
7. **London: UK sanctions Yemen's Houthis and four senior officials.**
   Asset freezes and travel bans. Source: Daily Summary, 24 Nov 2026.
8. **Nepal: magnitude 5.4 earthquake strikes Koshi Province.**
   Tremors are felt across India, Bhutan and Bangladesh.
   Source: The Statesman, 24 Nov 2026.
